    How the model works

    Times use Sri Lanka local time (Asia/Colombo). An hourly interval is wet when it records at least the selected rainfall threshold. An event is exposed if one or more of its hours are wet.

    Choose a start and compare its exact fitted risk with 13:00 under the same settings. The combined option samples October and November in proportion to their 31 and 30 days.

    The Wilson interval shows how much the simulated estimate can vary at a given trial count with the fitted probabilities held fixed.

    The model was fitted to October–November hourly ERA5 rainfall from 2016–2022. Use the historical risks for advance planning. Check a current forecast near the event date.

    Contains modified Copernicus Climate Change Service information. ECMWF ERA5 hourly data accessed via the Open-Meteo Historical Weather API.
